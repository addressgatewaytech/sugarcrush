About us

Made to be shared. Baked to be craved.

SC Bake House is the home-grown bakery brand of SugarCrush Trading W.L.L., based in Qatar. We started with a simple idea: a treat can be both wholesome and indulgent.

That is why our menu runs from better-for-you bakes such as our gluten-conscious millet brownies to rich, chocolate-loaded classics, traditional cardamom Nankhatai, café-style cookies, tiramisu and cheesecakes.

Every order is baked fresh, in small batches, with premium ingredients. For birthdays, weddings, Eid, National Day and corporate events, we design celebration cakes, gift boxes and full dessert tables around your theme and colours.

Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Because our products are perishable food made to order, they cannot be returned once delivered. If something is wrong with your order, we will make it right with a replacement, store credit or refund as set out below.

1. Cancelling an order

  • Regular bakes and desserts: free cancellation up to 24 hours before the delivery time.
  • Celebration cakes and custom orders: free cancellation up to 72 hours before delivery. Between 72 and 24 hours, 50% of the advance is refundable. Less than 24 hours: the advance is non-refundable, as ingredients and decorations have already been prepared.
  • Dessert tables and corporate orders: cancellation terms are stated in your written quotation.

2. Changes to an order

Changes to flavour, design, message or delivery time can be requested up to 48 hours before delivery and are subject to availability. Changes may affect the price.

3. Damaged, incorrect or missing items

  • Check your order on delivery. Report any issue on WhatsApp within 2 hours of delivery, with clear photos of the product and packaging.
  • Where the item is damaged in our delivery, incorrect, or missing, we will offer a replacement, store credit or a full or partial refund.
  • We cannot accept claims for damage after handover to a third-party courier arranged by the customer, or for issues caused by improper storage.
  • Taste is personal; dissatisfaction with taste alone is not grounds for a refund, but we always welcome your feedback.

4. How refunds are paid

  • Approved refunds are made to the original payment method only.
  • Card refunds are processed within 7 working days and may take 7–14 working days to appear, depending on your bank.
  • Bank transfer refunds are sent to the account that made the payment. Cash-on-delivery orders are refunded by bank transfer.

5. Orders we cancel

If we must cancel your order for reasons on our side (for example ingredient shortage or an emergency), you will receive a 100% refund of any amount paid.
